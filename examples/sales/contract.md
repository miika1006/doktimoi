# Northstar contract — synthetic fixture

## Cancellation

The customer must give written notice at least 60 days before the annual renewal date.
Early termination does not entitle the customer to a refund, except when the supplier materially breaches the agreement and fails to remedy the breach within 30 days of written notice.

## Data export

The customer may export its data for 30 days after termination. Exports use CSV or JSON.
The supplier deletes the remaining customer data after that period, unless a legal retention obligation applies.

## Availability

The service availability target is 99.9% per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance agreed with the customer.
This target is not a guarantee of uninterrupted service.
